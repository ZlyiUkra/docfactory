# версія: 2.10

> **InvalidDynamicRoute**: The INVALID_PARAM param for route ROUTE is invalid. Received **RECEIVED**.

## What went wrong?

A dynamic route param is invalid. This is often caused by an `undefined` parameter or a missing rest parameter.
