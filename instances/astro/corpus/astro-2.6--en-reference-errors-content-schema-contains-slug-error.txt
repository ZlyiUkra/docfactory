# Content Schema should not contain slug.
# джерело: https://github.com/withastro/docs/blob/ad666cb07cb3a95e34e4567a10b5f19b138feb8f/src/content/docs/en/reference/errors/content-schema-contains-slug-error.mdx
# отримано: 2026-09-21
# версія: 2.6

> **ContentSchemaContainsSlugError**: A content collection schema should not contain `slug` since it is reserved for slug generation. Remove this from your COLLECTION_NAME collection schema. (E09003)

## What went wrong?

A content collection schema should not contain the `slug` field. This is reserved by Astro for generating entry slugs. Remove the `slug` field from your schema, or choose a different name.
