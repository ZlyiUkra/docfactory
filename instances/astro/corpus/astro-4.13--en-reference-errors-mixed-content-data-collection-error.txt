# Content and data cannot be in same collection.
# джерело: https://github.com/withastro/docs/blob/2139f21685f62462b3df142735cf85c6db31f8b9/src/content/docs/en/reference/errors/mixed-content-data-collection-error.mdx
# отримано: 2026-09-21
# версія: 4.13

> **MixedContentDataCollectionError**: **COLLECTION_NAME** contains a mix of content and data entries. All entries must be of the same type.

## What went wrong?

A content collection cannot contain a mix of content and data entries. You must store entries in separate collections by type.
