# Invalid dynamic route.
# джерело: https://github.com/withastro/docs/blob/86eb0ea666efa35e7f130cc9ed9dd232da1965fb/src/content/docs/en/reference/errors/invalid-dynamic-route.mdx
# отримано: 2026-09-21
# версія: 4.6

> **InvalidDynamicRoute**: The INVALID_PARAM param for route ROUTE is invalid. Received **RECEIVED**.

## What went wrong?

A dynamic route param is invalid. This is often caused by an `undefined` parameter or a missing rest parameter.
