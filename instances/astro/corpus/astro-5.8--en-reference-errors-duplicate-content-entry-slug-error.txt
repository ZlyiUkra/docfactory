# отримано: 2026-09-21

> `COLLECTION_NAME` contains multiple entries with the same slug: `SLUG`. Slugs must be unique.

## What went wrong?

Content collection entries must have unique slugs. Duplicates are often caused by the `slug` frontmatter property.
