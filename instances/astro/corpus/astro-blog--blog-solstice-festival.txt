**The planets have aligned to bring you the **Astro Solstice Festival** – A week-long celebration of all things Astro.**

Running from **June 23rd to June 27th, 2025**, the Astro Solstice Festival was a series of exciting events, including live streams, community challenges, and more. Whether you're a seasoned Astro developer or just getting started, there's something for everyone. Don't worry if you missed the live events – you can catch up on all the content right here and on our YouTube channel.
