Return the full text of one excerpt of the BullMQ documentation by its identifier, together with the page or
file it belongs to, every version whose documents contain this text, the URL it was taken from and the date the
text was fetched -- so a quotation can be checked against the source even a year later.

Call this after `search_docs` when the excerpt came back cut at 600 characters, or when the exact wording
matters: a job or worker option and its default, a guide page, a Lua script that moves a job between states,
a Bull REFERENCE entry, a migration step, a release note, a test that shows the real behaviour.

Do not guess identifiers: they are not URLs and cannot be assembled by hand. Every identifier this tool
accepts comes from the `id` field of a `search_docs` result.

The text comes back in English. Answer the user in their own language and follow the rules of
`search_docs`: names stay in English, name the product, the version and the page you read.
