# Clerk Core 2 is now Generally Available
# джерело: https://clerk.com/changelog/2024-04-19
# отримано: 2026-09-25

After a little over a month in Beta, we're making our latest major release _Generally Available_. Core 2 represents a massive overhaul to the way Clerk works under the hood and ships with a bunch of goodies for you and your users. Going forward, whenever you install one of our Clerk packages, you'll be able to take full advantage of the latest Core 2 features.

We want to thank everyone who participated in the beta and helped us get to this point 💜.

Head to our Core 2 Upgrade Guides or try one of our Quickstarts to get started with Core 2 today!
