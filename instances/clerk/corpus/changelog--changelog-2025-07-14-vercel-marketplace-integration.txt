# Clerk is now available on the Vercel Marketplace
# джерело: https://clerk.com/changelog/2025-07-14-vercel-marketplace-integration
# отримано: 2026-09-25

Clerk is now available on the Vercel Marketplace in its new _Authentication_ category. With one-click setup, automatic environment variable sync, and unified billing through Vercel, it's easier than ever to integrate Clerk into your Vercel projects.

With the marketplace integration you can:

- Leverage all of Clerk's existing features, such as Organizations and Clerk Billing
- Create a Clerk account and spin up Clerk applications directly from the Vercel dashboard
- Sync your Clerk API keys into your Vercel project's environment variables
- Manage billing through your existing Vercel account

Get started with Clerk on the Vercel Marketplace.
