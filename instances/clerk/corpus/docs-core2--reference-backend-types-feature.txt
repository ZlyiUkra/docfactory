# `Feature`
# джерело: https://github.com/clerk/clerk-docs/blob/core-2/docs/reference/backend/types/feature.mdx
# отримано: 2026-09-25
# версія: core-2

SDK: js-backend

> [!WARNING]
>
> Billing is currently in Beta and its APIs are experimental and may undergo breaking changes. To mitigate potential disruptions, we recommend pinning your SDK and `clerk-js` package versions.

The `Feature` object represents a Feature of a Subscription Plan.

  - `id`
  - `string`

  The unique identifier for the Feature.

  ---

  - `name`
  - `string`

  The name of the Feature.

  ---

  - `description`
  - `string`

  The description of the Feature.

  ---

  - `slug`
  - `string`

  The URL-friendly identifier of the Feature.

  ---

  - `avatarUrl`
  - `string`

  The URL of the Feature's avatar image.
