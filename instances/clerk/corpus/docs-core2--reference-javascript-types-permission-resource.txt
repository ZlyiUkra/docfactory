# `PermissionResource`
# джерело: https://github.com/clerk/clerk-docs/blob/core-2/docs/reference/javascript/types/permission-resource.mdx
# отримано: 2026-09-25
# версія: core-2

SDK: js-frontend

An interface that represents a Permission in an Organization.

  - `id`
  - `string`

  The unique identifier of the Permission.

  ---

  - `key`
  - `string`

  The unique key of the Permission.

  ---

  - `name`
  - `string`

  The name of the Permission.

  ---

  - `type`
  - `'system' | 'user'`

  The type of the Permission.

  ---

  - `createdAt`
  - `Date`

  The date when the Permission was created.

  ---

  - `updatedAt`
  - `Date`

  The date when the Permission was last updated.
