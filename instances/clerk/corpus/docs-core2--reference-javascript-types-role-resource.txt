# `RoleResource`
# джерело: https://github.com/clerk/clerk-docs/blob/core-2/docs/reference/javascript/types/role-resource.mdx
# отримано: 2026-09-25
# версія: core-2

SDK: js-frontend

An interface that represents a Role in an Organization.

  - `id`
  - `string`

  The unique identifier of the Role.

  ---

  - `key`
  - `string`

  The unique key of the Role.

  ---

  - `name`
  - `string`

  The name of the Role.

  ---

  - `description`
  - `string`

  The description of the Role.

  ---

  - `permissions`
  - PermissionResource\[]

  The Permissions of the Role.

  ---

  - `createdAt`
  - `Date`

  The date when the Role was created.

  ---

  - `updatedAt`
  - `Date`

  The date when the Role was last updated.
