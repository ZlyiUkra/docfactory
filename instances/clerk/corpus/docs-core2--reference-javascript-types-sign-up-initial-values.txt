# `SignUpInitialValues`
# джерело: https://github.com/clerk/clerk-docs/blob/core-2/docs/reference/javascript/types/sign-up-initial-values.mdx
# отримано: 2026-09-25
# версія: core-2

SDK: js-frontend

The type for the `initialValues` prop that is used to pre-populate the sign-up form.

## Properties

  - `emailAddress?`
  - `string | undefined`

  The user's email address.

  ---

  - `username?`
  - `string | undefined`

  The user's username.

  ---

  - `phoneNumber?`
  - `string | undefined`

  The user's phone number in E.164 format.

  ---

  - `firstName?`
  - `string | undefined`

  The user's first name.

  ---

  - `lastName?`
  - `string | undefined`

  The user's last name.
