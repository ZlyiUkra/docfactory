* Use filepath instead of path to check if the dockerfile path is abolute or not by @glours in https://github.com/docker/compose/pull/8988
* Upgrade version of opencontainers/image-spec (security issue) by @glours in https://github.com/docker/compose/pull/8989
* Merge and fix Convert function from `docker/compose-switch` by @ulyssessouza in https://github.com/docker/compose/pull/8985
