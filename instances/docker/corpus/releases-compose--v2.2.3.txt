Tag v2.2.3, published 2022-01-06.

## What's Changed

* `compose images` should list images of created containers by @kiniou in https://github.com/docker/compose/pull/8990
* Ignore missing (swarm) overlay networks by @ndeloof in https://github.com/docker/compose/pull/8999
* Remove intermediate containers when build succeeded in classic build by @notok in https://github.com/docker/compose/pull/9012
* compose ps: fix typo "unknow" -> "unknown" by @webignition in https://github.com/docker/compose/pull/9017
* Add 2 modes test mechanism by @ulyssessouza in https://github.com/docker/compose/pull/9007
* Handle "stop" event by @ndeloof in https://github.com/docker/compose/pull/9019
* Only kill running containers by @ulyssessouza in https://github.com/docker/compose/pull/9020
* compose cp doesn't need a full project and can copy from stopped containers by @ndeloof in https://github.com/docker/compose/pull/9004
* Add dependabot by @ulyssessouza in https://github.com/docker/compose/pull/9036
* Do not try to guess when to allocate a TTY and keep it as default by @ulyssessouza in https://github.com/docker/compose/pull/9035
* Bump github.com/golang/mock from 1.5.0 to 1.6.0 by @dependabot in https://github.com/docker/compose/pull/9042
* Bump github.com/spf13/cobra from 1.2.1 to 1.3.0 by @dependabot in https://github.com/docker/compose/pull/9038
* Bump github.com/hashicorp/go-multierror from 1.1.0 to 1.1.1 by @dependabot in https://github.com/docker/compose/pull/9040
* Bump github.com/buger/goterm from 1.0.0 to 1.0.3 by @dependabot in https://github.com/docker/compose/pull/9047
* Propagate GroupAdd from ServiceConfig to HostConfig by @lancechentw in https://github.com/docker/compose/pull/8987
* Bump github.com/containerd/console from 1.0.2 to 1.0.3 by @dependabot in https://github.com/docker/compose/pull/9039
* Bump github.com/AlecAivazis/survey/v2 from 2.2.3 to 2.3.2 by @dependabot in https://github.com/docker/compose/pull/9048
* Added volume bind option SELinux label :z :Z by @tymonx in https://github.com/docker/compose/pull/9073
* Downgrade goterm to 1.0.1 by @ulyssessouza in https://github.com/docker/compose/pull/9080
