Return the full text of one excerpt of the dukascopy-node documentation by its identifier, together with the document
it belongs to, every version whose documents contain this text, the URL it was taken from and the date the text was
fetched from that URL.

Call this after `search_docs` when the excerpt you need came back cut (`truncated: true`), or when the exact wording
matters: the options of `getHistoricalRates` and their defaults, the flags of the CLI, the earliest date of an
instrument, the code of an example.

Do not guess identifiers: every identifier this tool accepts comes from the `id` field of a `search_docs` result.

The text comes back in English, as the documentation is written. Answer the user in their own language, keep
identifiers and titles in English, and name the version and the document you read.
