Return the full text of one excerpt of the golang-migrate documentation by its identifier, together with the document
it belongs to, every golang-migrate release whose documents contain this text, the URL it was taken from and the date
the text was fetched from that URL -- so a quotation can be checked against the source even a year later.

Call this after `search_docs` when the excerpt you need came back cut (`truncated: true`), or when the exact wording
matters -- a command and its flags, a database URL and its parameters, the signature of a function, a change listed in
the release notes; a rule quoted half-way is how a wrong answer starts.

Do not guess identifiers: they are not URLs, not page paths and cannot be assembled by hand. Every identifier this tool
accepts comes from the `id` field of a `search_docs` result.

The text comes back in English, as the documentation is written. Answer the user in their own language, and follow the
same rules as for `search_docs`: keep identifiers and titles in English, use Ukrainian words for Ukrainian prose, and
name the release and the document you read.
