Return the full text of one excerpt of the golang.org/x documentation (x/crypto argon2 and bcrypt, x/sync, x/time/rate)
by its identifier, together with the document it belongs to, every release whose documents contain this text, the URL
it was taken from and the date the text was fetched from that URL -- so a quotation can be checked against the source
even a year later.

Call this after `search_docs` when the excerpt you need came back cut (`truncated: true`), or when the exact wording
matters -- the declaration and contract of a function, the recommended parameters, a limit; a rule quoted half-way is
how a wrong answer starts.

Do not guess identifiers: they are not URLs, not page paths and cannot be assembled by hand. Every identifier this tool
accepts comes from the `id` field of a `search_docs` result.

The text comes back in English, as the documentation is written. Answer the user in their own language, and follow the
same rules as for `search_docs`: keep identifiers and titles in English, use Ukrainian words for Ukrainian prose, and
name the package and the release you read.
