Return the full text of one excerpt of the gVisor documentation by its identifier, together with the page or
file it belongs to, every release whose documents contain this text, the URL it was taken from and the date
the text was fetched -- so a quotation can be checked against the source even a year later.

Call this after `search_docs` when the excerpt came back cut (`truncated: true`), or when the exact wording
matters: a runsc flag with its default and help text (the Config struct and RegisterFlags are long Go
declarations), the usage and flags of a runsc subcommand, a Docker daemon.json or containerd config.toml and
runsc.toml example, a row of the syscall compatibility table, a supported NVIDIA driver or capability, a
checkpoint/restore option, a step of a tutorial, a netstack type, function or option from pkg.go.dev, a release
note, the runsc package entry of the APT repository.

Do not guess identifiers: they are not URLs and cannot be assembled by hand. Every identifier this tool
accepts comes from the `id` field of a `search_docs` result.

The text comes back in English. Answer the user in their own language and follow the rules of
`search_docs`: names stay in English, name the release (or say the page has no version) and the page you read.
