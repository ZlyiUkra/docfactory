with the reference of the languages and libraries the book is written in. The book teaches a systematic way to design
programs -- the design recipe: data definitions, signatures, purpose statements, examples, templates driven by the
shape of the data, definitions, tests -- in the teaching languages of Racket, run in DrRacket.
