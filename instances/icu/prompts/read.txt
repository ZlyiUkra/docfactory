Return the full text of one excerpt of the ICU documentation by its identifier, together with the page or
header it belongs to, every release whose documents contain this text, the URL it was taken from and the
date the text was fetched -- so a quotation can be checked against the source even a year later.

Call this after `search_docs` when the excerpt came back cut (`truncated: true`), or when the exact wording
matters: a function's signature, parameters and return values, its @stable/@draft/@deprecated status, an
option and its allowed values, the full text of a User Guide chapter section.

Do not guess identifiers: they are not URLs and cannot be assembled by hand. Every identifier this tool
accepts comes from the `id` field of a `search_docs` result.

The text comes back in English. Answer the user in their own language and follow the rules of
`search_docs`: names stay in English, name the release and the header or page you read.
