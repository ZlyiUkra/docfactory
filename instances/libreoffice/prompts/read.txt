Return the full text of one excerpt of the LibreOffice documentation by its identifier, together with the
page, section or file it belongs to, every version whose documents contain this text, the URL it was taken
from and the date the text was fetched -- so a quotation can be checked against the source even a year later.

Call this after `search_docs` when the excerpt came back cut (`truncated: true`), or when the exact wording
matters: a table of command line parameters, a filter name and its options, a Calc function with its syntax
and example, the steps of a help page, a UNO method with its parameters and exceptions, a property of
MediaDescriptor, a section of the Developer's Guide, a release note, a CVE advisory, a README of a wrapper, a
line of code.

Do not guess identifiers: they are not URLs and cannot be assembled by hand. Every identifier this tool
accepts comes from the `id` field of a `search_docs` result.

The text comes back in English. Answer the user in their own language and follow the rules of
`search_docs`: names stay in English, name the version, and the page or section you read.
