Return the full text of one excerpt of the libuv documentation by its identifier, together with the
document it belongs to, every version whose documents contain this text, the URL it was taken from and
the date the text was fetched -- so a quotation can be checked against the source even a year later.

Call this after `search_docs` when the excerpt came back cut (`truncated: true`), or when the exact
wording matters: a function's signature and return value, the order of loop phases, a note about
thread safety or a platform difference is how a wrong answer starts.

Do not guess identifiers: they are not URLs and cannot be assembled by hand. Every identifier this
tool accepts comes from the `id` field of a `search_docs` result.

The text comes back in English. Answer the user in their own language and follow the rules of
`search_docs`: names stay in English, name the version and the document you read.
