Return the full text of one excerpt of the MongoDB documentation by its identifier, together with the page it
belongs to, every version whose documents contain this text, the URL it was taken from and the date the text
was fetched -- so a quotation can be checked against the source even a year later.

Call this after `search_docs` when the excerpt came back cut (`truncated: true`), or when the exact wording
matters: the syntax of a method or command with all its fields, an operator's behavior table, a configuration
option and its default, a release note, a step of an installation or upgrade procedure.

Do not guess identifiers: they are not URLs and cannot be assembled by hand. Every identifier this tool
accepts comes from the `id` field of a `search_docs` result.

The text comes back in English. Answer the user in their own language and follow the rules of
`search_docs`: names stay in English, name the version and the page you read.
