There's one difference between regular guards and the websockets guards. The websockets guards takes the `data` passed from the client instead of expressjs request object as an argument of the `canActivate()` function. Also, when the guard returns `false`, it throws `WsException` (not `HttpException`).

Hint The `WsException` class is available within the `@nestjs/websockets` package.
