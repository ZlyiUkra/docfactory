There's no difference between regular interceptors and the microservices interceptors. Here is an example that makes use of a manually instantiated method-scoped guard (class-scoped works either):

```typescript
@UseInterceptors(new TransformInterceptor())
@MessagePattern({ cmd: 'sum' })
sum(data: number[]): number {
  return (data || []).reduce((a, b) => a + b);
}
```
