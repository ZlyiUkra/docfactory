Return the full text of one excerpt of the OpenSSL documentation by its identifier, together with
the page it belongs to, every release whose documents contain this text, the URL it was taken from
and the date the text was fetched -- so a quotation can be checked against the source even a year
later.

Call this after `search_docs` when the excerpt came back cut (`truncated: true`), or when the exact
wording matters: an option and its default, a function's signature and return values, the HISTORY
section that says since which release a function exists or was deprecated.

Do not guess identifiers: they are not URLs and cannot be assembled by hand. Every identifier this
tool accepts comes from the `id` field of a `search_docs` result.

The text comes back in English. Answer the user in their own language and follow the rules of
`search_docs`: names stay in English, name the release and the page you read.
