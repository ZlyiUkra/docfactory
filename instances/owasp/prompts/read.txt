Return the full text of one excerpt of the OWASP documentation (the Cheat Sheet Series, the
Top 10 of 2025, 2021, 2017 and 2013, the API Security Top 10 of 2023 and 2019, ASVS 5.0 and
4.0.3, the Proactive Controls of 2024 and 2018) by its identifier, together with the document it
belongs to, the URL it was taken from and the date the text was fetched from that URL -- so a
quotation can be checked against the source even a year later.

Call this after `search_docs` when the excerpt you need came back cut at 600 characters, or
when the exact wording matters -- an ASVS requirement, the order of defenses in a cheat sheet, a
header value or a password hashing parameter quoted half-way is how a wrong answer starts.

Do not guess identifiers: they are not URLs, not requirement numbers and cannot be assembled by
hand. Every identifier this tool accepts comes from the `id` field of a `search_docs` result.

The text comes back in English, as the documentation is written. Answer the user in their own
language, and follow the same rules as for `search_docs`: name the edition when it matters
(Top 10 2025, ASVS 5.0), keep category names, requirement numbers and titles in English, use
Ukrainian words for Ukrainian prose, and name the document you read.
