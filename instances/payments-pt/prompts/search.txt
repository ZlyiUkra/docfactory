Search the documentation of four Portuguese online payment providers: ifthenpay, Easypay, Eupago and SIBS (SIBS Pay and
the SIBS Gateway). The corpus holds, for ifthenpay: the OpenAPI references of MB WAY, Multibanco, Credit Card, Direct
Debit, Payshop, Pix, Pay by Link, refunds, callbacks and payment lists, the guides, its MCP server, the help center in
Portuguese, the site with its prices and solutions, the blog, the README of its plugins for WooCommerce-style form
builders and other platforms, the JavaScript SDK and the PHP SDK. For Easypay: the documentation in English and
Portuguese (payment methods, payment types, subscriptions, frequent payments, webhooks, changelog), the OpenAPI
reference, the site with its prices and complete price list, the blog and the checkout SDK. For Eupago: the REST API
reference, the site with its price list, payment methods, recurring payments, the page for institutions and associations
and the FAQ, and press news. For SIBS: the documentation of SIBS Pay, its Portugal part and its international part (MB
WAY, Multibanco references, cards, authorised payments, notifications, backoffice), the site and the news.

Use this tool for: fees and price lists of a provider, which payment method a provider supports, how to create an MB WAY
payment, a Multibanco reference or a SEPA Direct Debit mandate, callbacks and webhooks, recurring charges and
subscriptions, joining requirements for an association, plugin setup. Queries may be in English or Portuguese. Write the
provider's name and the page's own term in the query.

Do not use it for Stripe (a separate server holds it), for the law on data protection, or for banks' own conditions. The
site pages carry no version: prices are what the site showed on the fetch date, so give that date and tell the reader to
confirm a price with the provider. Blog and press pages are marketing, not contract terms. Returns the matching excerpts
with the source address and the fetch date.
