## formState

`formState` | `isDirty` | `boolean`

`dirtyFields` | `object`

`touchedFields` | `object`

`isSubmitted` | `boolean`

`isSubmitSuccessful` | `boolean`

`isSubmitting` | `boolean`

`submitCount` | `number`

`isValid` | `boolean` | Note: `isValid` is affected by `mode` at useForm. This state is only applicable with `onChange`, `onTouched`, and `onBlur` mode.

`isValidating` | `boolean`

`errors` | `object` | An object with field errors. There is also an ErrorMessage component to retrieve error message easily.
