## Why?

### HTML standard

Leverage your existing html markup and start validation your forms with standard validation.

### Super Light

Performance is important, and pack size matters. it is only 2k gzipped and without any dependencies.

### Performance

Minimises the volume that is triggered with component re-rendering, try to provide your users with the best experience.

### Adoptable

Since form state is inherently local, it can be easily adopted without other dependency.
