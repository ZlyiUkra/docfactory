# версія: 4.0.3

Tag v4.0.3, published 2015-12-22.

- Fixes a bug that caused the props of connected components to not updated during a hot update with something like React Hot Loader. Internally, it involved moving the memoized calculations from `shouldComponentUpdate()` into more appropriate places such as `componentWillReceiveProps()`, `handleChange()`, and `render()`. (https://github.com/rackt/react-redux/issues/224, https://github.com/rackt/react-redux/pull/225)
