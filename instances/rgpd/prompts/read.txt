Return the full text of one excerpt of the data protection law corpus by its identifier, together with the document it
belongs to, the URL it was taken from and the date the text was fetched from that URL -- so a quotation can be checked
against the source even a year later.

Call this after `search_docs` when the excerpt you need came back cut (`truncated: true`), or when the exact wording
matters -- a paragraph of an article, an exception, a condition of consent, a sanction; a rule quoted half-way is how a
wrong answer starts.

Do not guess identifiers: they are not URLs, not article numbers and cannot be assembled by hand. Every identifier this
tool accepts comes from the `id` field of a `search_docs` result.

The text comes back in the language of the document, Portuguese or English. Answer the user in their own language, keep
legal terms and quotations in the original, and name the document, the article and the paragraph you read. If the excerpt
opens with an OCR warning, say that the quotation must be checked against the PDF.
