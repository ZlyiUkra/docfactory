Search the data protection law that applies in Portugal. The corpus holds the General Data Protection Regulation
(RGPD in Portuguese, GDPR in English, Regulation (EU) 2016/679): its 99 articles and 173 recitals, in Portuguese and in
English; the Portuguese Lei n.º 58/2019, which executes the RGPD; the Portuguese Lei n.º 41/2004 in its current wording,
whose articles 13.º-A and 13.º-B govern unsolicited communications for direct marketing; Directive 2002/58/EC (ePrivacy),
which Lei 41/2004 transposes, in both languages; the guidance of the Portuguese supervisory authority CNPD (Comissão
Nacional de Proteção de Dados): its directives (Diretriz 1/2022 on electronic direct marketing), regulations and
deliberations since 2018 (Deliberação 2019/494 disapplies some articles of Lei 58/2019; Deliberação 2019/297 is a fine for
unsolicited email), its older guidance, and its pages for organisations and citizens; and three guidelines of the European
Data Protection Board (EDPB, CEPD in Portuguese): 05/2020 on consent, 07/2020 on controllers and processors and 1/2024 on
legitimate interest.

Call this when a question is about who may be written to and on what legal basis, consent and how to prove it, the
exception for existing customers, direct marketing to natural and to legal persons, the right to object, the contract
with a processor (Article 28), the record of processing activities (Article 30), duties of associations and other
controllers, data subject rights, breaches, fines, or what the CNPD has decided. Query in Portuguese, in English or both:
the texts of the laws are in Portuguese and the RGPD also in English; accents do not matter to the word search.

Do not call this for the tax rules of an association, payment providers, Portuguese law outside data protection, or the law
of other countries: it is not here. Decreto-Lei 7/2004 on electronic commerce is cited by these texts but its own text is
not here. Pareceres of the CNPD and its deliberations before 2018 are left out on purpose: they rest on the repealed Lei
67/98 or comment on draft laws.

Read a result with these limits in mind. The articles of Portuguese law come from the consolidated text of pgdlisboa.pt,
which the CNPD itself links to, not from the official Diário da República. A document that opens with an OCR warning was
read from a scan and has lost its accents and may have errors: check any quotation against the PDF at its source address.
Older CNPD guidance may predate the RGPD: say so and check whether it still holds. A deliberation decides one case; do not
present it as a general rule. Guidelines 1/2024 is the public consultation version. A proposal to amend the RGPD (the
Digital Omnibus of November 2025) is not in the corpus: the articles here are the consolidated text of 4 May 2016.

The results are the law as written, not legal advice: when the answer depends on facts, such as whether a member of an
association counts as a customer, name the article, say what it leaves open and that a lawyer or the CNPD should confirm.

Answer in the language of the question, quote the law in Portuguese with the article and paragraph, and answer a question
about several rules point by point, naming the document for each point.

The `search` field of the result says how the excerpts were found: "words" means the words of the query had to appear
in the text, so a query phrased in other words than the law uses may miss; "meaning+words" means a second, meaning-based
index answered as well, and a paraphrase had a chance.
