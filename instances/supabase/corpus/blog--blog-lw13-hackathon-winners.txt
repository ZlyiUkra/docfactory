# Blog: Supabase Launch Week 13 Hackathon Winners (2024-12-20)
# джерело: https://supabase.com/blog/lw13-hackathon-winners
# отримано: 2026-09-24

Launch Week 13 brought an incredible array of new Supabase features that got developers' creative engines revving. To harness this excitement, we challenged our amazing community with the Launch Week 13 Hackathon - and wow, did they deliver!

The submissions were truly impressive, showcasing exceptional technical skill and creativity. Our team thoroughly enjoyed reviewing each project, making it challenging to select winners from such a strong pool of entries. You can browse through all the submissions here. For this hackathon, we're excited to have collaborated with StackBlitz to offer special prizes for projects built with bolt.new! Now, let's meet the winners!

## Best overall project

### Winner

Brainrot GPT by Timmy, and Tiara

Brainrot GPT uses AI to turn PDFs into short form video summaries. Perfect if you have a sub-30 second attention span.

Brainrot GPT

### Runner Up

SupaSketch by Swayam bansal, and Anchal

SupaSketch: Sketch and compete with friends in an AI and Supabase powered multiplayer drawing challenge!

SupaSketch

## Best use of AI

### Winner

Stravation by Aaron Byrne

AI-driven motivation, BLE heart rate tracking, & real-time connectivity to power your runs.

Stravation

### Runner Up

Kontra by Tami

AI-powered contract analysis tool that detects risky clauses and protects you from unfair legal agreements.

Kontra
