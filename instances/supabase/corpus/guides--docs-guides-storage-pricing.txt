# Pricing
# джерело: https://supabase.com/docs/guides/storage/pricing
# отримано: 2026-09-24

You are charged for the total size of all assets in your buckets.

$0.00002919 per GB-Hr ($0.0213 per GB per month). You are only
charged for usage exceeding your subscription plan's quota.

| Plan       | Quota in GB | Over-Usage per GB | Quota in GB-Hrs | Over-Usage per GB-Hr |
| ---------- | ----------- | ----------------- | --------------- | -------------------- |
| Free       | 1           | -                 | 744             | -                    |
| Pro        | 100         | $0.0213           | 74,400          | $0.00002919          |
| Team       | 100         | $0.0213           | 74,400          | $0.00002919          |
| Enterprise | Custom      | Custom            | Custom          | Custom               |

For a detailed explanation of how charges are calculated, refer to Manage Storage size usage.

Caution: If you use Storage Image Transformations, additional charges apply.
