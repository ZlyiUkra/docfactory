# Lost access/Forgot the MFA device
# джерело: https://supabase.com/docs/guides/troubleshooting/lost-accessforgot-the-mfa-device-nAPT-7
# отримано: 2026-09-24

**Non-SSO User**

For now, we will not be able to restore access to your account if you lose all your two-factor authentication credentials. Register a backup factor if necessary.

If you forgot the MFA authentication method used, you can reach out to our support.

**SSO User**

Reach out to our support and we will assist you further.
