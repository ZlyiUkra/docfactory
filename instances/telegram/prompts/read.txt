Return the full text of one excerpt of the Telegram developer documentation by its identifier, together with the
document it belongs to, the URL it was taken from and the date the text was fetched from that URL.

Call this after `search_docs` when the excerpt you need came back cut (`truncated: true`), or when the exact wording
matters: the parameters of a method and which of them are required, the fields of a type, a limit, a code example.

Do not guess identifiers: every identifier this tool accepts comes from the `id` field of a `search_docs` result.

The text comes back in English, as the documentation is written. Answer the user in their own language, keep method,
type and field names in English, and name the document you read.
