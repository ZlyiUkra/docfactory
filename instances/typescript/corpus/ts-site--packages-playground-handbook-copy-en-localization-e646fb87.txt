## Localization

Like the rest of this website, the TypeScript Playground is localized. As of 2021, the current list of supported lanaguges are:

- `es`
- `fr`
- `id`
- `ja`
- `ko`
- `pt`
- `vo`
- `zh`

Ideally this helps folks for whom English is a second language!
