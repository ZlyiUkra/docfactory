Tag v0.0.3, published 2026-09-02.

## Breaking

- The `visibility` option on `new Bucket()` and `Bucket.fromEnv()` is gone. Whether a bucket is private is decided in the console; the SDK now reads it from the credentials response and defaults to public when the backend does not say. A private bucket has no `url` or `versionedUrl` on any BlobObject and stores `Cache-Control: private`.
- `bucket.publicUrl(path)` is now async: it fetches credentials once to know whether the bucket has a public host.
