# версія: blob-0.0.4

Tag v0.0.4, published 2026-09-03.

- `copy` and `move` take `{ contentType, cache, metadata }`. Any one given rewrites all three on the destination; the others are carried over from the source.
- `updateJson` takes `maxAttempts` (default 6) and pauses with jittered backoff between rounds.
- Renamed `overwrite` to `allowOverwrite` and `maxBytes` to `maxSize`.
