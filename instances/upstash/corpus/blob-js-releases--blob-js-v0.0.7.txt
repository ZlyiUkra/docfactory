# версія: blob-0.0.7

Tag v0.0.7, published 2026-09-26.

Signed URLs (`signedReadUrl`, `signedUploadUrl`, upload handler PUT and multipart parts) are now signed by Upstash (`/v1/presign`), no longer with the bucket's temporary credential, which anyone holding a URL could use on the whole bucket.

URLs last at most 10 minutes: a longer `expiresIn` is shortened, and the upload default drops from 1h to 10 minutes. Paths with an empty segment (`dir/`, `a//b`) are refused for signing.

PR: https://github.com/upstash/blob-js/pull/7
