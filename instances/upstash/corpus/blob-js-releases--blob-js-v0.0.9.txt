Tag v0.0.9, published 2026-10-01.

`uniquePath` keeps IDs and filenames exactly as given (case, spaces, Unicode, slashes) and only adds the random suffix. The upload handler answers a traversing path (`../x.png`) with 400 `invalid_input` instead of a 500.

Size options accept binary units: `32MiB` = 33,554,432 bytes, `32MB` stays 32,000,000. Reads and multipart completion keep the stored `Content-Length` and ETag; invalid lengths are rejected instead of read as 0.

PR: https://github.com/upstash/blob-js/pull/4
