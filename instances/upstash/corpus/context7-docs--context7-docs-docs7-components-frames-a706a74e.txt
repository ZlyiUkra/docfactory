Wrap visual content in `<Frame>`.

## Usage

```mdx
<Frame caption="A framed preview">
  <div style={{ padding: "3rem", textAlign: "center" }}>
    Screenshot or video
  </div>
</Frame>
```

Use `width` to constrain the frame or `fullWidth` to fill the content column.
