## What's Changed

### Fixes

- **Prevent event loss during history replay** (#24, fixes #23): `subscribe({ history: true })` could silently drop events published while the `XRANGE` history replay was in flight, because the pub/sub `message` listener was only registered after replay finished. Listeners are now registered up front; live events arriving during replay are buffered, delivered after the historical entries, and deduplicated against the last replayed stream id — including on the post-replay pub/sub path, since `emit` runs `XADD` before `PUBLISH` and the same event can arrive through both.
- Fixed a leaked ping interval when a subscriber unsubscribes while history replay is still running.

### Internal

- Added a `bun test` regression suite covering the replay race, both deduplication paths, and the unsubscribe-during-replay case.

Thanks @smorimoto for the report and fix!
