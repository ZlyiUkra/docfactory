# Deprecation Notice
# джерело: https://upstash.com/docs/kafka/connect/deprecation
# отримано: 2026-10-16

As of April 2024, Kafka Connectors are deprecated and will be removed in October, 1st 2024. Please check our blog post for more information.

If you were previously using Kafka Connect provided by Upstash, please follow this guide to migrate to your own self-hosted Kafka Connect.

If you have any questions or need further assistance, reach out to us at support@upstash.com or join our community on Discord.
