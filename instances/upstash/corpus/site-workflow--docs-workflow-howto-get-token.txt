# Workflow: Get your QStash Token
# джерело: https://upstash.com/docs/workflow/howto/get-token
# отримано: 2026-10-16

Upstash Workflow is powered by QStash, so your app
authenticates with a QStash token. Go to the
Workflow tab in the Upstash Console, pick
the region you want to use, and copy the `QSTASH_TOKEN` from the **Quickstart**
section.

Info:
If you want to safely share a token with your team or AI agents, you can use a
read-only token instead.
