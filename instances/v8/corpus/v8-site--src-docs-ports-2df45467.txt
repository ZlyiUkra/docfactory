# Handling of ports
# джерело: https://github.com/v8/v8.dev/blob/031a6578036da033a0c3fb728ec58527345336b6/src/docs/ports.md
# отримано: 2026-09-29
# версія: 15.4, 2026, 15.3, 15.2, 15.1, 15.0

This document describes how ports should be handled.

## ARM

For straight-forward ARM ports, do them yourself. If there are difficulties or questions, contact the ARM team in the CL by adding `v8-arm-ports@googlegroups.com` to the reviewer or CC list. For questions you can email the group (mailto link).

## MIPS (not officially supported)

The Mips team will do the port. Contact/CC the MIPS team in the CL if needed by adding `v8-mips-ports@googlegroups.com` to the reviewer or CC list. For questions you can email the group (mailto link).
