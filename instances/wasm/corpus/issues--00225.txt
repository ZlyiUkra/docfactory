# WebAssembly spec issue #225: ml-proto does not accept the full range of unsigned i32 or i64 constants
# джерело: https://github.com/WebAssembly/spec/issues/225
# отримано: 2026-09-30

Issue #225, opened by ghost on 2016-02-01, closed (completed) on 2016-06-21.

The ml-proto does not appear to support unsigned i32 or i64 constants with the high bit set, rather requires them to be signed numbers. Both wasm-as and sexp-wasm accept either signed or unsigned.
