# WebAssembly spec issue #495: [spec] Grammar A+ (mistake?)
# джерело: https://github.com/WebAssembly/spec/issues/495
# отримано: 2026-09-30

Issue #495, opened by pyfisch on 2017-06-04, closed (completed) on 2017-06-04.

The Grammar section defines A+ as:

> A+ is a possibly empty sequence of iterations of A. (This is a shorthand for An where n≥1.)

I think this is a copy-paste error and it was meant to say:

> A+ is a sequence of iterations of A with at least one element. (This is a shorthand for An where n≥1.)
