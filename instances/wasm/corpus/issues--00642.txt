In the WAST grammar `nat` is not defined.

Could you please confirm me that `nat: <int>` is correct, I would be happy to PR.

## Comment by rossberg, 2018-01-08

Nat is a natural number, just like int is an integer number. I didn't bother to add all the lexical syntax before, but I just added it.
