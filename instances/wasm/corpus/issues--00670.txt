# WebAssembly spec issue #670: [bikeshed] Vertical alignment of arrays
# джерело: https://github.com/WebAssembly/spec/issues/670
# отримано: 2026-09-30

Issue #670, opened by rossberg on 2018-02-13, closed (completed) on 2022-08-04. Labels: editorial.

Some uses of arrays do not seem to respect the [t] placement, such that vertical alignment is off.

(For example, see the grammer definition for store here.)

## Comment by flagxor, 2018-04-10

This is due to [t] etc. not being supported:
https://github.com/Khan/KaTeX/issues/269

We could make this less glaring if we changed this:
```
   \begin{array}{llll}
   store &::=& \{~
     \begin{array}{lll}
     SFUNCS & funcinst^\ast, \\
     STABLES & tableinst^\ast, \\
     SMEMS & meminst^\ast, \\
     SGLOBALS & globalinst^\ast ~\}
     \end{array}
   \end{array}
```
