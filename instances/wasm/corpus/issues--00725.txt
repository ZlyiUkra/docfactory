## Comment by liminzhu, 2018-03-27

Did an attentive pass on intro and a rough pass on the rest of the core spec for consistency issues. Overall the spec is in very good shape! Some feedback:

* ~~https://github.com/WebAssembly/spec/issues/759~~
* nit - there are lots of arbitrary uses of colon or period before bullet points or formulas. (e.g. 1 2 3 4). Maybe we can use colon in places where phrases like 'the following' or 'as follow' are used immediately before points or formulas and use period otherwise, or just apply some general principle. Certainly low pri as it doesn't really affect readability or anything much.
* nit - If a footnote is displayed as a paragraph longer than a line, the number is placed next to the center of the paragraph rather than the start (example), which is a bit odd. Not sure if this is a bikeshed limitation or not.
