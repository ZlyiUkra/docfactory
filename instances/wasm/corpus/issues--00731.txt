That section relates to things that aren't really written about in any other specification that I know of. It's a good question whether it's really normative or not. Unlike the ECMAScript specification, which has many introductory sections that are considered "obviously" non-normative, web specs tend to explicitly note whether text is normative or not. This text is not noted as non-normative, but I'm not sure what it would really mean given the lack of specs to hook into. Do you think it should be marked as non-normative given tat?
