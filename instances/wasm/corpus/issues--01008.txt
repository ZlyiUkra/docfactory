Found here.

`locals` is said to be a compressed vector however it shows `t:valtype` rather than `(t:valtype)ⁿ` which mismatches against `vec(B)` definition.

Definition of `locals`
locals
Definition of `vec(B)`
vector

I don't understand this and I think it might be a typo.

## Comment by rossberg, 2019-04-29

`locals` decodes into a sequence of n locals of the same type t. A `func` in turn contains a vector of those, whose results get concatenated to a single AST vector.

## Comment by Galaxtone, 2019-04-30

Ah, so to put is simply, `(n=3 t=i32) (n=2 t=f32)` would become `(i32, i32, i32, f32, f32)`?

## Comment by rossberg, 2019-04-30

Yes.
