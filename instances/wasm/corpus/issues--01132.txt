# WebAssembly spec issue #1132: i32.const -1 violates the formal grammar
# джерело: https://github.com/WebAssembly/spec/issues/1132
# отримано: 2026-09-30

Issue #1132, opened by hexagonrecursion on 2020-02-17, closed (completed) on 2020-02-17.

Call me pedantic, but the definition of const

> instr ::= ... | i*nn*.const i*nn* | ...

implies

> instr ::= ... | i32.const i32 | ...

and i32 as defined here

> i*N* ::= u*N*
u*N* ::= 0 | 1 | ... | 2^*N* -1

implies

> i32 ::= 0 | 1 | ... | 2^32 -1

which *technically* means that the use of `i32.const -1` in the definition of memory.grow violates the formal grammar.
