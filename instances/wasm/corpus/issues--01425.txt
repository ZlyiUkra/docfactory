# отримано: 2026-09-30

Issue #1425, opened by codefromthecrypt on 2022-02-25, closed (completed) on 2022-08-04.

I find some things confusing to know what was added after MVP. The W3C permalink has an explicit ref to `REC-wasm-core-1-20191205`. However a date encoded permalink isn't precise as that could be the publication date, days or weeks after something was submitted, and the also it could be on another branch. If we had a tag `REC-wasm-core-1-20191205` here, it would be easy to know which things were merged afterwards.

Most notably, I can't really understand if global import/export of mutable globals are post MVP as there's some mixed language, ex https://www.w3.org/TR/2019/REC-wasm-core-1-20191205/ discusses the ability to export definitions after mentioning globals can be mutable, and unlike return values, there's no constraint mentioned. That doesn't mean they are supported, just I am confused and I think if there was a git tag directly matching that version of the href, I could simply see if #814's commit happened after it.
