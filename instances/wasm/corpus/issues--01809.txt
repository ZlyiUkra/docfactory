# WebAssembly spec issue #1809: Text format typos

# отримано: 2026-09-30

Issue #1809, opened by verbessern on 2024-09-21, closed (completed) on 2024-09-21.

In the instructions text format there are missing: extadd_pairwise_i16x8_u, and for the f64x2/f32x4 the ceil, floor, trunc, and nearest.

The fNxM.add instruction in the change history vector instructions list is separated from its "group" with fNxM.sub, not only in this proposal.

Also the ordering of the instructions in the binary and the text sections is a bit different in some groups of instructions, just saying.
