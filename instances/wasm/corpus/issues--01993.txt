## Comment by tabatkins, 2025-09-30

Ah, yeah, this sort of issue can happen due to the relative timing of macro processing vs includes/excludes. (Macros are done first, and aren't exclude-aware.) There's an easy fix, one sec.

## Comment by tabatkins, 2025-09-30

Okay, fix pushed - I marked the two DEADLINE macros as allowed to silently fail. Deadline is already a required metadata for CRs, so it'll still be caught correctly there if you're missing it.

However, I note that the wasm boilerplate also uses the DEADLINE macro in its CRD paragraph, but Deadline isn't a required metadata for CRDs in general. Is it intentional to use DEADLINE there, or should y'all tweak your boilerplate a bit?

## Comment by dschuff, 2025-09-30
