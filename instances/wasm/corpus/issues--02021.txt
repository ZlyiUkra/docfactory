I've found some incorrect spacing at the bottom of  Vector Instructions in the Binary Format section. All the spaces need to be underscores `_` as a single instruction.

> | `0xFD` 252:`u32` ⇒ `i32x4.trunc_sat_s`**HERE**`zero_f64x2`
> | `0xFD` 253:`u32` ⇒ `i32x4.trunc_sat_u`**HERE**`zero_f64x2`
> | `0xFD` 254:`u32` ⇒ `f64x2.convert_low`**HERE**`s_i32x4`
> | `0xFD` 255:`u32` ⇒ `f64x2.convert_low`**HERE**`u_i32x4`
> | `0xFD` 257:`u32` ⇒ `i32x4.relaxed_trunc_s_f32x4`
> | `0xFD` 258:`u32` ⇒ `i32x4.relaxed_trunc_u_f32x4`
> | `0xFD` 259:`u32` ⇒ `i32x4.relaxed_trunc_s`**HERE**`zero_f64x2`
