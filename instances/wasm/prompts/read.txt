Return the full text of one excerpt of the WebAssembly specification or of one issue
discussion by its identifier, together with the document it belongs to, every edition
whose text contains this excerpt, the URL it was taken from and the date the text was
fetched from that URL -- so a quotation can be checked against the source even a year
later.

Call this after `search_docs` when the excerpt you need came back cut at 600 characters, or
when the exact wording matters -- a validation or execution rule, an algorithm step of the
JavaScript Interface, a binary encoding or a grammar production quoted half-way is how a
wrong answer starts.

Do not guess identifiers: they are not URLs, not section numbers and cannot be assembled by
hand. Every identifier this tool accepts comes from the `id` field of a `search_docs` result.

The text comes back in English, as the specification is written. Formulas are written out
as text: an inference rule reads "premises ⟹ conclusion", "t_1" is a subscript, "t*" a
sequence, "t?" an optional item. Answer the user in their own language, keep identifiers
and section titles in English, and name the edition and the section you read.
