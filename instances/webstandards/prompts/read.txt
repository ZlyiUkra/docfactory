Return the full text of one excerpt of the web standards (WHATWG, W3C, IETF RFCs, OpenID
Connect, OpenAPI, JSON Schema, ERC) by its identifier, together with the document it
belongs to, the URL of the section it was taken from and the date the text was fetched --
so a quotation can be checked against the source even a year later.

Call this after `search_docs` when the excerpt you need came back cut at 600 characters, or
when the exact wording matters -- the steps of an algorithm, a MUST or SHOULD, the grammar
of a header, the conditions of a WCAG success criterion or the keyboard model of an APG
pattern quoted half-way is how a wrong answer starts.

Do not guess identifiers: they are not URLs, not section numbers and cannot be assembled by
hand. Every identifier this tool accepts comes from the `id` field of a `search_docs`
result.

The text comes back in English, as the standards are written. Answer the user in their own
language, and follow the same rules as for `search_docs`: keep identifiers, requirement
keywords and titles in English, use Ukrainian words for Ukrainian prose, and name the
standard and the section number you read.
